Fund the Kid

Investor home

Every American wants better education. They disagree on how best to deliver that education.

Those disagreements are an asset. Fund the Kid puts every idea on the table: public, private, charter, micro, extra help, for-profit, and nonprofit. No gatekeeper. No thumb on the scale. The brand promise is a true open field and a dispassionate look at outcomes as the people giving and running the work define those outcomes.
